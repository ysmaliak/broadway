Broadway 0.1.2

New in this version

  • A smaller mini player. One row in the sidebar with the artwork, the title and the keys. Mute Tab and Hide Player are in its menu.
  • Looks. Noir is black. Dusk is brighter, Borealis is new, and the Glass tile shows real glass.
  • Folders. A folder you make sits in the tab list where you made it. A folder inside a folder lines up with the tabs beside it.
  • Live folders. A live folder shows it is loading right after you sign in.
  • New Tab. An empty New Tab closes itself when you leave it.
  • Search suggestions. They are on unless you turn them off in Settings › Field.
  • Polish. The sidebar, Settings, History, Downloads, Bookmarks, Passwords and Bring Things Over share one set of measures. The words across the app are shorter and plainer.
  • Fixed. The space icon at the foot of the sidebar could turn black on a dark sidebar. The main button on a bar looked switched off in a window that was not in front.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

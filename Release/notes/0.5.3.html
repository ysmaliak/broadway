Broadway 0.5.3

New in this version

  • Agents by space. The Agents card in the sidebar shows the agents at work in the space you are in, with only the tabs they hold there. An agent with no tab yet shows in the space that was in front when it connected. When an agent in another space needs you, that space's icon at the foot of the sidebar carries a small dot until you answer or go there.
  • The agent's face has eyes only. The small brows are gone. An agent that needs you looks up with large round eyes, and one that could not finish has small eyes held low.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Broadway 0.1.1

New in this version

  • Sign in from a folded live folder. A live folder that is waiting for a sign-in or single sign-on keeps its button when it is folded, so you can sign in without opening the folder.
  • Fixed. New Tab in another stacked space is the first thing drawn there, not that space's last tab. The selected tab at the end of a list keeps its whole shadow.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Broadway 0.6.2

Fixed and improved

  • Agents open in the right space. An agent's session goes to the space that fits its project folder: one you moved that folder's sessions to before, or one named in the folder's path, such as Personal for a project under a personal folder. Only then the space on screen. Its later tabs stay in that space, and the agent is told where it opened and how to choose another with broadway open URL --space NAME.
  • The Agents card's session menu has Move to Space, and Broadway remembers the choice for that folder.
  • The Agents card moves with its space. When you swipe between spaces, each space's card slides in and out with it instead of staying behind and popping.
  • No jumps after a space switch. The space that slides in looks exactly as it does once it lands, and the list no longer scrolls on its own afterwards.
  • Close buttons show only on the row under the pointer, even while you scroll, and a folder's count no longer overlaps its close button.
  • Selected tab shortcut. The button that scrolls back to the selected tab is now off by default. Turn it on in Settings › Sidebar. It now shows the selected tab's row pinned to the edge of the list.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

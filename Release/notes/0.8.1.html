Broadway 0.8.1

Fixed and improved

  • The sidebar lines up. The keys at the top stand over the address's keys, the address starts on the line of the icons, a tab in a folder sits under the folder's name, pinned tiles have even gaps, and the player's keys end where the Install button ends.
  • A tick in a menu sits with the symbols. A live folder's menu and the translate menu keep one column for the tick and the symbols, so the words no longer step in.
  • A video that starts late still floats. Leave a tab before its video has begun and it floats once it plays, on any site.
  • Tabs you carry together land together. Several tabs dropped at once settle as one block.
  • Sharper marks. The play and pause mark sits in the middle of its key on every screen, and the arrow that turns when you pull to reload no longer wobbles.
  • Agents. A ref from a page the tab has left is refused, with a line that tells the agent to take a new snapshot.
  • Smaller things. A folded space with no tabs shows no count. The Developer Mode question names the site first. A long title in the ⌘T field's History, Downloads and Bookmarks keeps its room when its row is selected. No Recent Downloads and the find bar's keys are easier to read. Pages keep no gap between them during a swipe. With one space, history from before 0.3 is placed in it.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

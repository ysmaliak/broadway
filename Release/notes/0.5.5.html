Broadway 0.5.5

New in this version

  • Calls open in the right space. Settings › General › Calendars has a new Spaces for calls section. Pick a space for each calendar account, or for a single calendar, and its video calls open there. Left on Any Space, a call opens in the space its account is signed in to, or where its calls opened before. Broadway learns which account a call belongs to from Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Meet. Move a call to another space by hand and Broadway offers once to always open that account's calls there.

Fixed and improved

  • The update card's close button sits inside the window instead of on its edge.
  • The Close, Open and Beside buttons beside a link preview stay inside a small window with a wide sidebar.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Broadway 0.5.1

Fixed in this version

  • Glass stays glass on everything that floats. The floating video, the call window and meeting alerts keep their clear glass when Broadway is not the app in front. Before, they went flat and grey there.
  • Play and pause swap with one small motion. On a floating video and on the player in the sidebar, the old mark shrinks away and the new one grows back in its place.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

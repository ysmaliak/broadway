Broadway 0.7.3

New in this version

  • All downloads open in the Command-T window. Show All Downloads, the Downloads command and its shortcut open the Command-T window on Downloads instead of a tab. Downloads that are running, paused or failed come first with their progress, then your files, and typing filters both. Pause, resume, retry, cancel, Show in Finder, Copy Path and Remove all work from the keyboard, and each row has its own menu.
  • About Broadway is the standard macOS About window.

Fixed and improved

  • The meeting alert's Join key is one clean shape in the system green. A square plate could show behind its rounded ends.
  • Download progress holds still. The numbers no longer shake sideways while a download or an update comes in, and a size keeps its length at a round value.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

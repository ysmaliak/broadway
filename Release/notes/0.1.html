Broadway 0.1

Broadway is a browser for the Mac built on WebKit and drawn in AppKit. This build is signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple.

New in this build

  • A better mini player. The title has the whole width, the time and the progress line have a row of their own, and the card shows on the playing tab too. Speed is offered for long audio only, and the clock follows what the page is really playing.
  • Update Ready. When an update has downloaded, a card at the foot of the sidebar offers Restart. The same action is in the Broadway menu and in Settings › General.
  • Live folders in one place. Settings has no Accounts page any more. Settings › Live Folders offers the folders you can add, and Morning Brief is under Tabs.
  • Contact Support. Settings › General › Help and the Help menu start a mail to support@broadwaybrowser.com with your version filled in.
  • Fixed. Picking a tab in another stacked space no longer slides the page in from the side. The window buttons go grey when the window is not in front. The downloads list opens above its button with a clean pointer. The Settings header keeps its size when you scroll. The Codes from Messages switch is gone, because codes fill in without it.

In Broadway

  • Spaces. Each keeps its own tabs, pins, folders and colour, and can have sign-ins of its own.
  • A sidebar that keeps up. Tabs, folders, and a live folder that follows the pull requests waiting on you.
  • Split view. Two pages side by side in one window.
  • Floating video and calls. A video or a meeting stays in view while you work in other tabs.
  • For you and your agents. A broadway command line that reads and drives the browser you are signed in to.
  • Updates. Broadway keeps itself up to date. You can turn that off in Settings.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

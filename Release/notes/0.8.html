Broadway 0.8

New in this version

  • Back and Forward survive a restart. Every tab that is not private comes back with its Back and Forward list and its place on the page, whether it was awake or asleep when you quit.
  • Drag several tabs at once. Tabs you picked with ⌘ or ⇧ travel together, with a count on the row you carry. Let them go on a folder, the pins, another space or between other tabs.
  • Closed windows come back. ⇧⌘T reopens a closed window with its tabs, and History › Recently Closed lists it as Window with 3 Tabs. Close Other Tabs and Close Both Pages each come back in one step.
  • Numbers that count roll. Minutes left, minutes ago, call clocks and count chips roll their changed digits the way the address does when you change tabs.
  • More of a tab's title. A long title runs closer to the end of its row and gives way to the close button when the pointer arrives.
  • A page can't hold a window with dialogs. From its second dialog in a row an alert offers Don't let this page show more dialogs.
  • Site permissions in one list. Settings › Site Permissions shows every answer you gave a site, allowed or refused, each with its own Remove…. Camera and microphone are two answers, and a site that has one is asked only for the other.
  • Another space's menu without leaving yours. Right-click a space's dot or its heading to rename it, move it or change its icon. A folder moves to another space, or a new one, from its own menu.
  • Find keeps its search. Find Next and Find Previous work after the bar is closed, and Escape clears the marks on the page.

Fixed and improved

  • The floating call. Dragging or pointing at the floating call no longer shows the page's own pill over the tiles. The call and the floating video stay up on ⌘H, and the call flicks to a corner like the video.
  • Meetings. Joining by any door takes the meeting's alert down. Zoom and Teams meetings open in their apps from the calendar pin and Join Next Meeting too. Calendars you switched off no longer feed the pin, and it reads the calendar again when the Mac wakes.
  • Tabs and Split View. Moving a Split View by menu or key moves both pages. A page that crashes in a split reloads. A question a page asked in the background moves with its tab. A private tab keeps a site's zoom while it lives.
  • The address is the same everywhere. The pill and the row name where a load you started is going, and the page on screen while a page loads something by itself. A page that didn't load names the address that failed.
  • Live folders. The sidebar offers the same list as Settings and never adds a folder twice. Removing one from the sidebar asks first, deleting a space removes its folders, and a private window marks nothing as opened.
  • History and bookmarks. Searching finds every word in any order, the same way in the ⌘T field and on the pages. Forgetting one page can be undone. Clear History also clears the list of files given to sites, and asks first from the field.
  • Passwords. A password is kept for its site's port. An import never replaces a password you used more recently. An export that misses some says how many, and its file is never readable by others. Removing one password asks first.
  • Bringing things from another browser. A second import counts only what is new. Firefox profiles left out for a primary password are named, and every passwords file ends with a reminder to delete it.
  • Shortcuts. A key Broadway can't give says who uses it. A box that is listening can be read in light and dark, and stops listening when you click elsewhere.
  • Extensions. Access given while an extension runs is kept across restarts and updates, and its question lists everything it asks for. User scripts survive an update. A popup opens from the button you pressed. The toolbar button's menu matches Settings. An extension row says when it is starting or couldn't update.
  • Blocking. Settings says when the filter lists couldn't update. Page not working? names an extension that still blocks.
  • Agents. A tab you keep is yours: the agent's next command no longer acts in it. A question about removing something names the thing. In Ask mode a download's file is asked for like the download. A slow client can't stall Broadway. wait goes up to 60 seconds and takes a title or an element's state. AppleScript reads tabs only with Allow Scripts to Drive Broadway on.
  • Updates. Settings says updates are checked every four hours, and says when a check couldn't be made.
  • Smaller things. Unit answers tell Mb from MB. Bars at the foot of the window stay on their page. Copy Clean Link keeps the link's title. Leftovers of the removed assistant are gone.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

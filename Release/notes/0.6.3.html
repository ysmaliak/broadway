Broadway 0.6.3

Fixed and improved

  • The whole call in the floating window. A call lifted out of its tab now shows everyone the full page shows, in the same grid, however small the window is. Before, a small window showed only one person.
  • The floating call window's Back to Tab and Close buttons are round again over a bright picture.
  • Time left on a call stays readable. In a meeting's last minutes, the sidebar's "minutes left" sign no longer runs into the tab's title. The title fades out before it in every state, and the sign stands clear of the tab number when you hold ⌘.
  • A video with a picture no longer takes the sidebar's mini player.
  • The Open Link In chooser is the same size as the floating field, and longer lists scroll under its heading.
  • A pull request row that turns merged is washed in merge purple again.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

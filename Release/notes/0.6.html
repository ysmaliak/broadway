Broadway 0.6

New in this version

  • More comes over from Arc. Bringing things over from Arc now also brings your Air Traffic Control rules, as rules for links from other apps that open them in the same space, and gives each new space its Arc colour. The last screen says how many rules came over and how many were left out because Broadway has no place for them, such as rules for Little Arc.
  • Cookie notices are hidden. The ad blocker now also hides cookie notices, with the EasyList Cookie List. Nothing is accepted or refused for you. Turning blocking off for a site on its card shows them there again.
  • Find the tab you're on. When the selected tab is scrolled out of the sidebar, a small capsule with its icon shows at that edge. Click it and the list glides back to the tab.
  • New tab opened. A tab that opens in the background while the sidebar is hidden puts up a short note with Show, which takes you to it. Several in a row are counted in one note.
  • The sidebar over a full-screen video. Rest the pointer at the edge of the screen and the sidebar slides out over the video, which keeps playing. Escape or moving away puts it back. In a full-screen window, Escape now puts the slid-out sidebar away without leaving full screen.
  • More for a finished download. Right-click a download in the downloads list, History › Downloads or Files for Open With, Copy File and Share. ⌘C copies the selected file.
  • Copy Last Freeze. Broadway keeps a note on this Mac of the last 20 times it stopped answering for two seconds or more, with no address, title or typing in it. Help › Copy Last Freeze copies the latest, ready to paste into a GitHub issue.
  • Paste and Search. Paste and Go is called Paste and Search when what you copied is words to search for rather than an address.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

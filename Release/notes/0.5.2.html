Broadway 0.5.2

New in this version

  • Hide draft review requests. A live folder of pull requests can leave out the ones that ask for your review while they are still drafts. Choose Hide Draft Review Requests in the folder's menu. Your own drafts and drafts assigned to you still show through Created by You and Assigned to You, and a hidden draft appears on the next update once it is ready for review. For the command and for agents the same choice is broadway live drafts.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Broadway 0.5.4

New in this version

  • Agents fold like folders. With two or more agents at work, each agent's heading in the Agents card is the tab it is on, and its other tabs fold under it with a count, as a folder's do. Hover a heading to close that agent's tabs. When an agent needs you, its heading turns amber and shows the question. The card now comes and goes with the sidebar's own motion.
  • Agent tabs close when the session ends. However an agent's session ends, its tabs close in every space, except a tab you are looking at, which stays yours. A session with no command for 20 minutes ends by itself.
  • A merge joins in place. When a pull request is merged, its row draws two branches joining where its icon was.
  • Stacks catch up. When you load, reload or act on a pull request's page, its row and its stack in the sidebar follow within seconds, including pull requests that were just combined.

Fixed and improved

  • Every notice at the foot of the window comes and goes with the same rise and fade, and each kind goes away after its own time. Its time pauses while the pointer is over it or the window is in the background.
  • The comment sign on a pull request row sits at the row's end when there is no dot, and slides aside for the close button on hover.
  • The floating video's play and pause key swaps its mark the way the sidebar player does, without a jump.
  • The music player and update cards in the sidebar are clear glass again and take the space's colour.
  • Back, forward and reload fade with the slide when you switch spaces or tabs.
  • Site icons no longer blink in the sidebar when you switch spaces.
  • The pull to refresh arrow turns around its own centre.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

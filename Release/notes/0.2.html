Broadway 0.2

New in this version

  • Automatic folders. Broadway now sorts tabs by what they are about, so pages from different sites on one subject land in one folder. It runs on your Mac. If you add your own OpenRouter key in Settings › Tabs, OpenRouter sorts and names your folders instead. Only tab titles, site names, authors, folder names and searches are sent, never addresses or pages, and the key stays in your Keychain.
  • Spaces. A space's colour is now One Colour or Light and Dark, on a shorter card with a preview of each side, twelve colours and one Intensity slider. The rest is under More. With many spaces, the row at the foot of the sidebar stays inside the sidebar and follows the space in front. Scroll over it to bring any space into view.
  • Agents. The agent card at the foot of the sidebar is glass like the player and eases open and closed. The tab an agent works in wears the agent's colour round its page, and "An agent is working here" shows at the bottom with the other notices.
  • Dragging tabs. A dragged tab is one lifted picture under the pointer. The list stays still and a line shows where the tab will land. Over the pins it turns into a tile.
  • Back, forward and refresh. Swiping back or forward and pulling to refresh show the same glass disc. Its ring fills as you pull and closes when letting go will act.
  • Bring your things over. The first-run step offers Safari, Chrome, Dia and Arc, and starts on your default browser when it is one of them. Safari comes in from its own export file, with a button that opens Safari for you.
  • The brief is gone. The morning brief page has been removed, along with its settings and the weather it fetched.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

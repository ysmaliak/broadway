Broadway 0.1.4

New in this version

  • The mini player. A click on the progress line moves the line to that spot smoothly and keeps it there. The time no longer flashes the old position, the play button no longer flickers while the page seeks, and a long title slides back to its start when you move away. A track with large artwork no longer makes the player stutter.
  • Folders. A tab dragged out of a folder can be dropped among the loose tabs, at the top or the end of the list, and on either side of a folder. A line shows where it will land.
  • Updates. Broadway checks for a new version every four hours.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Broadway 0.7.2

Fixed and improved

  • The Agents card scrolls cleanly. Rows run to the card's rounded bottom edge, and at the top they fade and blur under the heading instead of meeting a hard line. A list that fits no longer scrolls on its own, and the sidebar scrolls instead. While you scroll, and for a moment after, an agent's tab changes no longer move the list.

Requires macOS 27 on a Mac with Apple silicon.
